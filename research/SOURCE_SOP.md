# Source SOP

Prefer first-party convention, venue, organizer, ticketing, airline, hotel, and vendor sources for operational claims. Secondary/community sources are valuable leads and corroboration, not silent replacements for attribution.

For each material claim capture:

- stable source locator and source owner;
- publication, effective, event, and retrieval dates where applicable;
- exact supporting text or private artifact pointer;
- access/health state;
- normalized claim and confidence;
- contradictions, freshness limit, and supersession link.

Never infer `confirmed` from repetition alone. Treat inaccessible, stale, unknown, tentative, changed, canceled, contradicted, personally confirmed, observed onsite, and superseded as explicit results. Retain recoverable evidence without placing every captured detail in the default interface. Private confirmations, travel details, contacts, receipts, location history, and screenshots must eventually live in private Storage; tracked files must not contain them.
