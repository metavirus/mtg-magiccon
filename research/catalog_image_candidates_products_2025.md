## Validation and rejected near-matches

- The four recommended direct assets returned HTTP 200 with image content types on 2026-08-29. Local read-only inspection confirmed dimensions and visible product identity: Fblthp mug 2048×2048, Commander Buddy 600×600, Atlanta Quiver 800×800, and Atlanta baseball cap 800×800.
- The Beeline Shopify product metadata returned one product image and identified Beeline Creative, Inc. as vendor. A four-page live manufacturer-catalog scan returned FBLTHP but no Owlbear entry.
- The MagicCon 2025 Las Vegas catalog is authoritative for Owlbear Cub Geeki Tiki, Fblthp keychain, and Fblthp bucket-hat existence, but its single merchandise-grid page is not a clean product-image replacement.
- Rejected: Cantrip `Mountaintop` candle/wax melts for `Cantrip Candle - Mountain`; full-size Fblthp or Loot Squishables for the Fblthp keychain; Atlanta baseball-cap image for the separately priced Atlanta snapback; resale-market imagery for all items.

## Promotion recommendation

Promote only the four **EXACT** direct assets after the normal visual review. Leave the five **NOT FOUND** records on source evidence with a clear `presentation image unavailable` state; enlarging or padding their noisy board crop does not improve the evidence.
