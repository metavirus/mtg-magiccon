# Addendum: uncertain 2025 headwear image options

Retrieved and visually inspected: 2026-08-29 (America/Los_Angeles)

Scope: exact-item alternatives for the Fblthp Bucket Hat and the MagicCon: Atlanta 2025 Baseball Cap. Different events, years, and neighboring hat SKUs are rejected even when visually similar.

## Fblthp Bucket Hat

### Candidate 1 — Hareruya retail listing

- Provenance: **reputable specialist retailer**, not first-party.
- Page: [Hareruya — Fblthp bucket hat](https://www.hareruyamtg.com/ja/products/detail/180244)
- Direct image: [240×192 PNG](https://files.hareruyamtg.com/product/685cc797b698e281.png)
- Exactness cues: the page explicitly says this Fblthp bucket hat was sold at MagicCon: Las Vegas 2025. The inspected photo shows the distinctive turquoise plush/fuzzy bucket construction, two side ears, and one large white-and-blue eye centered on the crown. Those features agree with the exact item's official 2025 MagicCon catalog thumbnail.
- Visual usefulness: clean front view with the whole product visible and no catalog typography, but low resolution and a dark decorative background. It is a real improvement for identity over a blurred board enlargement, not a high-resolution hero asset.
- Uncertainty: **low identity / high quality limitation**. The retailer listing is sold out and does not identify the original soft-goods manufacturer.

No second exact standalone image met the inspection bar. The eBay listing titled `MagicCon Las Vegas 2025 Exclusive Fblthp Bucket Hat` is useful corroboration, but its direct gallery asset could not be reliably resolved and inspected; it is therefore not promoted as a candidate.

## MagicCon: Atlanta 2025 Baseball Cap

### Candidate 1 — eBay resale listing, flattened official-style image

- Provenance: **resale evidence**, not first-party. Seller `Central Florida Anime` showed 100% positive feedback and 12K items sold when retrieved.
- Page: [eBay — Magic MTG MagicCon Atlanta 2025 Key Art Tibalt Baseball Cap Hat](https://www.ebay.com/itm/177441864329)
- Direct image: [799×799 JPEG](https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/ZtsAAeSwPQ9o1t89/s-l1600.jpg)
- Exactness cues: the inspected image shows the teal cap, peach/Planeswalker `MAGICCON` front embroidery, orange `ATLANTA 2025` interior tape, and Tibalt key art across the underside of the brim. These are the same four details described and pictured in the official [Atlanta 2025 merch preview](https://www.mtgfestivals.com/global/en-us/magiccon-news/magiccon-atlanta-merch-preview.html). The listing identifies Wizards of the Coast, 2025, and the Atlanta event.
- Visual usefulness: the same two-view product composition as the official PNG, but flattened against solid white. Inspection found none of the official PNG's severe striped/halo alpha-edge artifacts. Resolution is effectively the same (799 px versus 800 px).
- Uncertainty: **low identity / medium provenance**. This is a reseller-hosted copy, apparently derived from the official render rather than an independent product photograph. It should be mirrored only if the project's asset-rights policy permits use of reseller-hosted copies.

No second exact artifact-free candidate met the inspection bar.

## Rejected images

- [Official Atlanta baseball-cap PNG](https://www.mtgfestivals.com/content/dam/sitebuilder/rna/mtgfestivals/global/_images/press-releases/2025/mc-atlanta-merch-preview/mc-baseball-cap.png): authoritative and exact, 800×800, but visually rejected because the alpha boundary contains conspicuous horizontal color streaks and halos.
- [Official Atlanta 2025 preorder snapback](https://conv-prod-app.s3.amazonaws.com/media/med/65/4/96/c605c987-5762-44e4-9c22-e0f744fad889.png): clean 499×500 first-party commerce asset, but it is the separate black $35 Atlanta logo snapback, not the teal $40 baseball cap.
- MagicCon Chicago, Las Vegas, 2026 Atlanta, Brain Dead x Magic, and generic one-eyed bucket hats: different event/year/product variants.
- Official Las Vegas 2025 merchandise PDF: authoritative for the Fblthp bucket hat's identity, but the bucket-hat artwork is trapped in a dense catalog composite and offers no cleaner standalone presentation image.

## Practical conclusion

- **Bucket hat:** the Hareruya image is the only inspected exact-item standalone option found. It is honest but small; do not upscale it into a large hero panel.
- **Baseball cap:** the 799×799 eBay JPEG is an exact, visually clean substitute for the corrupted-alpha official PNG, subject to provenance/rights review.
