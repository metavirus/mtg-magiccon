Search-indexed first-party media exposed exact lanyard and tote product renders from `conv-prod-app.s3.amazonaws.com`. Both direct objects were reloaded successfully on 2026-08-29 and returned 500×500 PNGs.
- **Wizards of the Coast / Magic.gg:** the May 6, 2025 Atlanta announcement names “MagicCon 2025 Event Deck Box and Sleeves” in the Premium Weekend and Black Lotus VIP benefits. Its first-party image search result exposes the 1770×1770 2025 Yargle deck-box render from Wizards' Contentful domain; the direct object was reloaded successfully on 2026-08-29.

## Focused fuzzy logo-merchandise pass (2026-08-29)

Searches combined the event identity with product-noun and visual variants rather than relying on full catalog names: `Atlanta`/`ATL`, `MagicCon 2025`, `patch`, `embroidered patch`, `badge`, `pin`, `enamel pin`, `peach`, `planet`, `astronaut`, and `Fblthp`. Results were checked against the original 3072×4080 [onsite board photograph](https://i.redd.it/pw91blv8dirf1.jpg), especially the $15 patch and $10 logo-pin cells.

The pass recovered a clean, transparent-background-style event-logo asset from the first-party 2025 MagicCon Atlanta site: [499×399 AEM PNG](https://mcatlanta.mtgfestivals.com/content/dam/sitebuilder/rna/mtgfestivals/mc-atlanta/2025/images/framework/mc-atlanta-logo.png/_jcr_content/renditions/original.image_file.499.399.file/431859945/mc-atlanta-logo.png). It loaded successfully at 499×399 on 2026-08-29. Its orange peach, orbital ring, `MAGICCON ATLANTA` wordmark, and Fblthp astronaut match the motif visible on the board’s patch/pin group. This supports a **representative artwork** classification, not an exact physical-product classification.

Rejected fuzzy results included:

- MagicCon Atlanta **2026** logo pin/patch assets: wrong year and a different jagged-metal visual identity.
- MagicCon Chicago/Las Vegas 2025 pins, mana-symbol pins, Lost Tavern pins, and character pins: wrong city or different product family/artwork.
- Generic peach, astronaut, and Fblthp pins/patches: visual-word matches without MagicCon Atlanta provenance.
- Secondary marketplace listings for Atlanta character and mana-symbol pins: useful evidence that Atlanta pin merchandise circulated, but they do not depict the board’s logo pin and are not canonical presentation media.

## Promotion recommendation

Promote the exact lanyard and logo tote immediately. The 2025 deck-box render is first-party and likely exact, but compare it once against the board crop because the Atlanta board shortens the official “Deck Box and Sleeves” name. For the logo patch and logo pin, the first-party clean 2025 event-logo PNG is a defensible **representative artwork** fallback when the choice is between that and a noisy board enlargement; label it as representative and retain the board as the physical offer/price evidence. Keep board-derived presentation crops for the other unresolved items until an exact first-party render is recovered; do not substitute 2026 Atlanta, Chicago, Las Vegas, or generic event-logo artwork.
