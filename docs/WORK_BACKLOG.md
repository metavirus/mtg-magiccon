## Semantic Map miniproject

Canonical plan: `docs/SEMANTIC_MAP_MINIPROJECT_PLAN.md`. Active semantic model: `docs/MAP_SEMANTIC_DATA_CONTRACT.md`. Arrival procedure: `docs/MAP_INGESTION_RUNBOOK.md`. “Safe now” permits only contracts, quarantined historical/synthetic fixtures, tooling, and rehearsal. “Must wait” forbids every Atlanta 2026 spatial assertion until a first-party original passes the arrival runbook. Historical Atlanta 2025 is structural reference only; its user-confirmed west-up orientation is never inherited by 2026.

**Scope decision (2026-08-23):** pathfinding, directions, origin detection, camera-assisted positioning, accessibility routing, route simulation, and route-aware itinerary behavior are deferred as one cohesive later project. Current Map work is limited to an excellent browsable/searchable spatial surface with zoomed zone details and object popovers. The topology lessons remain useful for faithful entrances, walls, aisles, and relative placement, but no route UI or route graph is active scope.

**Runtime boundary (2026-08-24):** all speculative Map UI/runtime scaffolding is parked. The existing app keeps its current honest Map landing; no historical fixture, prototype renderer, map-specific source module, or prototype CSS may remain wired into `src/` while we wait for the official 2026 artifact. Durable research, contracts, raw imports, and validation fixtures remain under `docs/map-data/` and the backlog. This prevents dormant Map work from dirtying shared application files or slowing unrelated UI iteration.

The venue-level destinations are also deliberately considered but deferred: Official Show Store, Main Stage/Lost Tavern, and the Omni should become a compact higher-level context once final 2026 venue artifacts establish their actual buildings, floors, and relationships. They are ripe for work at map arrival, not missing from the design.
