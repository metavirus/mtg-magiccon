Prize Wall

Prizes, Prize Tix, & the Prize Wall!

What is the Prize Wall?

Have you ever gone to an arcade, played some fun games and received tickets, then redeemed those tickets for something cool before you left? That is exactly what the Prize Wall is!

What are Prize Tix and How Do I Get Them?

Prize Tix are awarded by participating in scheduled & on demand Magic Ticketed Play events that can later be redeemed at the Prize Wall for amazing items! Prize Tix payouts are listed in the description of each play event.

Where is the Prize Wall & When is it Open?

The Prize Wall is located inside the Magic Ticketed Play Area 

Hours of Operation:

  • Friday and Saturday: 10:00 AM to 11:59 PM
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
    • NOTE: You must be in line by 5:30 PM on Sunday

What is available on the Prize Wall?

  • Play Boosters starting at 500 Prize Tix
  • Collector Boosters starting at 2,500 Prize Tix
  • Commander Precons starting at 5,000 Prize Tix
  • Bundles starting at 6,000 Prize Tix
  • 12” x 17” ‘Jumbo’ Cards starting at 20,000 Prize Tix
  • 23” x 32” 'Oversized’ Cards starting at 40,000 Prize Tix
  • Uncut Sheets starting at 50,000 Prize Tix

Note: This list is not exhaustive, and a full list of prizes will be available. Other products may include:

  • Deck Boxes & Sleeves
  • Playmats
  • Dice
  • Secret Lairs
  • Cool Magic Related Items
  • AND MORE!  

Prize Tix have no cash value. Prize Tix are only valid for the duration of the MagicCon they were issued at and cannot be saved for or redeemed at future MagicCon events. All Prizes and Prize Tix costs are subject to change, and allocation is based on availability. Some limitations may apply.
